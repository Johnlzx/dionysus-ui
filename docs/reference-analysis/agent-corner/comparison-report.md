## Accepted differences

- Source hero imagery and product copy are replaced with a token-derived ASCII signal and Dionysus utility copy.
- The reference’s stronger multicolor bloom is reduced to the design system’s success/brand signal colors.
- Window motion is matched by spatial behavior and perceived rhythm, not exact decoded pixels or source easing data.

## Verification interval

- Source timeline: 0.5s full-duration sampling.
- Fast async window: 0.1s sampling from 36.0–42.5s.
- Candidate: state-based screenshots plus live pointer, focus, keyboard, history, send, stop, and maximize checks.
